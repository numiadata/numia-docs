# Token Pulse

![Token Pulse](/media/token-pulse.gif)

Token Pulse is Numia's real-time token tracking and analytics platform that provides comprehensive visibility into your token's on-chain activity. Designed for blockchain projects and Layer 1 networks, Token Pulse goes beyond traditional price charts to reveal the underlying behaviors driving your token's market dynamics.

## Overview

Token Pulse tracks every token movement across exchanges, monitors whale activity, and analyzes holder behavior patterns in real-time. This enables you to identify market sentiment shifts and strategic opportunities before they become apparent in price action.

## Key Features

### Real-Time Token Tracking
Monitor all token movements as they happen across exchanges and wallets. Track inflows, outflows, and trading activity with up-to-the-second accuracy.

### Holder Behavior Analysis
Understand how different wallet segments interact with your token:
- **Retail Wallets** (< $1K) - Track sentiment shifts from smaller holders
- **Mid-Size Holders** - Monitor accumulation and distribution patterns
- **Whale Activity** - Identify large holder movements and strategic positioning

### Sentiment Detection
Capture market sentiment in real-time by analyzing on-chain behaviors:
- Bridging activity and cross-chain movements
- Buying patterns during market dips
- Staking activity and long-term holder commitment
- Net positive/negative position changes by wallet segment

### Exchange Flow Monitoring
Track token movements to and from exchanges to identify:
- Selling pressure from exchange deposits
- Accumulation signals from exchange withdrawals
- Trading volume patterns across platforms

## Why Token Pulse Matters for Your Project

### Identify Trends Early
Spot sentiment shifts and behavioral changes before they're reflected in price charts. Token Pulse reveals the "why" behind price movements by showing actual holder actions.

### Make Data-Driven Decisions
Base your strategic decisions on comprehensive holder behavior data rather than price speculation. Understand whether your community is accumulating, distributing, or holding steady.

### Monitor Token Health
Track key metrics that indicate ecosystem health:
- Holder distribution and concentration
- Active vs. passive wallet ratios
- Long-term holder growth
- Exchange vs. wallet holdings

### Respond to Market Dynamics
React quickly to changes in holder behavior with real-time alerts and monitoring. Identify when retail sentiment is turning positive or when whales are repositioning.
